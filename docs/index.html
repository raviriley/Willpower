Take Control of Your Attention

Willpower is a macOS app that gives you the willpower to resist distractions and focus on what matters.

Download for macOS

Requires macOS 14.0 or later

Features

Willpower is a Mac app to block access to distracting websites so you can focus on what matters.

Blocklists

Make a list of distracting websites and block them all at once. Or pick from our premade ones.

Schedules

Automatically block sites during certain hours. Set it and forget it.

Visit Limits

Block sites after too many visits. Supports all modern browsers.

Locked Blocks

Can't be bypassed until time's up, even if you restart your computer or delete the app.
